## LINKEDIN POST

Amazon just built a dashboard to run your Walmart and TikTok Shop orders.

Inside Amazon's own software.

On September 24, at its Accelerate seller conference in Seattle, Amazon announced multichannel selling tools inside Seller Central. Connect your eBay, Shopify, TikTok Shop and Walmart accounts. See every order, from every channel, in one place. Fulfill across channels. See profitability by product, across channels, in one view.

Free. Rolling out gradually to US sellers over the coming months.

Amazon's own stated reason: more than 95% of its independent sellers already sell somewhere else too. That's not new information. It's been true for years.

So why solve it now?

Here's my read: this was never really about saving sellers a few hours reconciling spreadsheets. It's about becoming the operating layer underneath every channel a seller touches — including the ones built to compete with it.

That's not automatically a bad trade. Rithum and Linnworks have sold this exact convenience for years. Shopify's own Marketplace Connect already runs the reverse — Amazon and Walmart orders, managed from inside Shopify.

Consolidating tools isn't the problem. Where the data ends up living is.

Before connecting Walmart or TikTok Shop into Amazon's dashboard, I'd want three answers first:

What your Walmart and TikTok Shop seller agreements actually say about routing your order and sales data through a competing platform's software.

Whether "profitability per channel" gets calculated the same way everywhere — or leans on how Amazon defines cost on channels it doesn't operate.

Who ends up with the clearer real-time view of how you're performing on Walmart and TikTok Shop — you, or the company you're now handing that performance data to.

If you're running all three platforms right now, this is worth watching closely before you flip the switch. Not because it's dangerous. Because "free" tools built by your biggest channel are rarely just about your convenience.

Would you connect your Walmart and TikTok Shop accounts into Amazon's Seller Central? Curious where operators land on this.
