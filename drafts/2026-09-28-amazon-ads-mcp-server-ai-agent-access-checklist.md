## LINKEDIN POST

Amazon's flagship ad conference opened in San Francisco today.

The product on every stage isn't a new ad format. It's an AI agent that can act on your ad account, not just report on it.

Amazon's Ads MCP Server — open beta since February, front and center again at unBoxed this week — lets you connect Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or a custom-built agent directly into your account's API. Not a suggestion queue. Direct access.

Once connected, that agent can create, pause and delete campaigns. Move budgets and bids. Pull reporting. Touch billing and financial data, depending on the scope you grant it.

That's a real shortcut for the tedious parts of account management. It's also a decision most sellers and agencies are making by default, without ever deciding it on purpose.

Before you connect anything to a live ad account, settle four things first:

1. Scope it to one account, one marketplace — not your whole book of business through a single key.

2. Separate read from write. Querying, drafting, analysis — most of what people actually want an agent for — never needs write access at all.

3. Require human approval before anything executes: budget increases, bid changes, campaign deletion. No blanket automation on day one.

4. Confirm the connection logs every action, so you can trace exactly what changed and when, if something goes wrong while you're asleep.

If an agent burns budget on the wrong keyword overnight, Amazon isn't the one accountable for it. You are.

Worth five minutes in your next account review — before "let's connect an AI agent" becomes the default answer instead of a decision.

Comment if you've already connected one to a live account. Curious what scope you actually gave it.
