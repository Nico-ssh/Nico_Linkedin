## LINKEDIN POST

TikTok Shop's Black Friday campaign has an entry test.

Most sellers find out they failed it when the signup gets rejected — not before.

To qualify for BFCM 2026 campaign placement, three numbers have to clear the bar at the same time:

→ Account Health Rating above 150
→ On-time dispatch rate of at least 80%
→ Seller-fault cancellation rate at or below 5%

Miss one, and the application doesn't go through.

Getting approved isn't the finish line either. Shop performance gets rechecked while the campaign is live. Drop below the line mid-event, and TikTok can pull your benefits mid-event too.

Then there's the inventory deadline — a separate problem from eligibility. Current guidance has stock needing to be inbound to Fulfilled by TikTok warehouses by October 15, about two weeks from today, to be positioned for the Nov 27–Dec 1 window. An eligible shop with late inventory still misses the event.

For sellers running TikTok Shop behind Amazon or Walmart as a secondary channel, this is the one marketplace where account health isn't background hygiene. It's a literal gate on Q4 revenue.

Pull your Account Health Rating, dispatch rate and cancellation rate this week. If one is close to the line, you have about two weeks to fix it — not two weeks to decide whether to look.

(Thresholds and scoring have shifted more than once this year — confirm the live numbers in your own Seller Center before you plan around them.)

Comment or DM if you want a second set of eyes on where your shop stands before the 15th.
