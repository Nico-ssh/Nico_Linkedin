## LINKEDIN POST

Amazon just put an expiration date on two of your ad reporting tools.

It lands December 31 — right as you're closing out your biggest quarter and building next year's budget off that data.

Here's what's actually happening:

Amazon opened "unified reporting" in beta at unBoxed last November — one system pulling Sponsored Ads and DSP data together instead of two separate report builders. It went generally available in June. Since then, the legacy Sponsored Ads reports page and the legacy Amazon DSP reports page have been running in parallel, business as usual.

That parallel period ends December 31, 2026. Both legacy pages are being retired.

The part worth checking this week:

→ Scheduled reports you built in either legacy page don't carry over on their own. You rebuild them in the new Reporting section (Measurement and Reporting → Reporting) yourself.

→ Amazon has said an automated migration tool is coming. No ship date attached to that promise. Waiting for it means betting your Q4 reporting on a tool that might land in December — or might not.

→ If an agency, freelancer, or internal analyst built your dashboards or client reports off exports from either legacy page, that pipeline breaks on the same schedule, whether or not the person who built it still owns the account.

The upgrade itself is a real improvement — Sponsored Ads and DSP in one report instead of two, 15 months of daily history, six years of monthly. That's not the issue.

The issue is that "runs fine today" and "still runs in January" are two different claims, and the gap between them is a manual rebuild most people won't do until a report silently stops showing up.

If you're running Sponsored Ads or DSP and haven't opened the new Reporting tab yet, that's worth ten minutes before Q4 planning locks in — not after a report you needed for a year-end review doesn't send.
