## LINKEDIN POST

Amazon just became an ad reseller for OpenAI.

On September 10, Amazon Ads opened a pilot letting select US advertisers buy ChatGPT ad inventory — through Amazon DSP.

Not through OpenAI's own ad platform. Through Amazon's.

Strip away the "AI ads are here" headlines and here's what's actually in the pilot:

→ Amazon DSP handles the buying relationship. OpenAI still decides how and where those ads show up inside ChatGPT.
→ Ads run as sponsored text and image units beneath ChatGPT's answers, bought on a cost-per-click or CPM basis.
→ Amazon auto-generates ad assets straight from advertisers' product feeds — the same feed-based approach it already runs across Sponsored Display.
→ It's small and US-only for now. Delta Vacations is one of the few named advertisers in it so far.

The detail worth sitting with isn't "ads in ChatGPT."

It's that Amazon didn't build a ChatGPT ads product. It positioned DSP as the checkout counter for someone else's ad inventory.

That's the same pattern Amazon has been building toward all year: DSP as the one login you use to buy media, regardless of who owns the screen the ad lands on.

For most sellers reading this, nothing changes today. It's a limited pilot with a short advertiser list.

But if your DSP account has only ever run Amazon.com retargeting and product ads, this is worth noticing: the tool you're already paying for is expanding faster than most people's strategy for using it.

Worth asking in your next ads review: is DSP still just a remarketing line item for you — or is it becoming your actual media-buying layer? If you're not sure which one it is yet, that's exactly the conversation worth having before your 2027 budget gets set.
