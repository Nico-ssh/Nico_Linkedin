## LINKEDIN POST

In August, shoppers who clicked into a retail site from an AI chat tool converted 48% higher than everyone else on that same site.

That's not a forecast. That already happened, a month before the biggest shopping weekend of the year.

Adobe's 2026 holiday report, out September 28, builds on that number. It projects AI-referred traffic to US retail sites growing roughly 130% this season — spiking 159% on Thanksgiving, 95% on Black Friday, 88% on Cyber Monday. Still a small slice of total traffic. Still the fastest-growing slice. Still the one converting best.

Here's what most sellers are missing while they read that stat and move on.

Amazon's shopping assistant isn't the "Rufus" everyone tested for fun in 2024. It was quietly renamed Alexa for Shopping in May, and the way it reads your listing changed with it — treating your bullets and title as a set of claims, then checking those claims against your actual reviews and Q&A before deciding whether to recommend you. Walmart went further and put a real storefront inside ChatGPT back in March.

Both platforms are already built for this traffic. Most listings are still written for the old search bar — keyword in the title, keyword in the bullets, done.

Before Black Friday, pull your top 5 ASINs and check three things:

→ Does the bullet copy actually answer the questions buyers ask, or just repeat the keyword?
→ Do your reviews and Q&A support every claim your listing makes?
→ If you read your own listing out loud as a summary, would it sound like something worth recommending?

The traffic growing fastest this quarter doesn't care how your SEO looks. It cares whether your listing holds up as an answer.

What are you seeing in your own AI-referral traffic so far this quarter? Genuinely curious if it matches what the reports are saying.
