## LINKEDIN POST

Amazon just gave Black Friday sellers three more weeks.

It didn't give them three more weeks of inventory.

October 20 was supposed to be the last day to submit a Black Friday/Cyber Monday deal. On October 23, Amazon reopened the window — multiple sellers and agencies are now reporting a Seller Central update pushing deal submission out to November 13.

Good news. Mostly.

Here's the actual split:

WHAT MOVED
→ Deal submission deadline: Oct 20 → Nov 13
→ Time to fix a deal that got blocked by a pricing, performance, or promotion-history check

WHAT DIDN'T MOVE
→ AWD inbound cutoff: Oct 14 (already gone)
→ FBA minimal-split cutoff: Oct 21
→ FBA Amazon-optimized-split cutoff: Oct 28

If your stock wasn't already moving toward a fulfillment center on the original schedule, a later deal deadline changes nothing. You can get a flawless, fully-approved Lightning Deal through the dashboard — and still have no inventory in the building to sell against it when the deal goes live.

This extension genuinely helps one group: sellers who had inventory positioned correctly but got stuck on a pricing or performance check before the 20th. For everyone else, "more time to submit" is not the same thing as "more time."

One more thing worth checking before you relax: reports disagree on whether Lightning Deals and Best Deals are both covered through Nov 13 for every seller and ASIN. Open your own Deals dashboard and read your specific deal type's status — don't assume it from a blog post. (Including this one.)

This weekend's real audit isn't the deal dashboard. It's the inbound shipment screen.

What are you seeing on your end — did Nov 13 show up in your account, or are you still showing Oct 20?
