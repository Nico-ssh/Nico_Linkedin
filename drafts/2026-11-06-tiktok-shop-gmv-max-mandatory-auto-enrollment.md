## LINKEDIN POST

TikTok Shop is about to spend your ad budget for you.

Not with a notification asking first. Automatically — starting November 11, unless you check one setting before then.

Here's the mechanic, per TikTok's own Seller University documentation on BFCM 2026 campaign registration:

To run in TikTok Shop's Black Friday/Cyber Monday campaign (Nov 11–30), every approved product has to sit inside an active GMV Max ad campaign. That's no longer a choice you opt into.

If a product doesn't have one running, TikTok creates it for you — 7 days before the campaign opens. Your account. Your budget. A campaign nobody on your team configured.

For a lot of sellers, TikTok Shop has felt like two separate lanes: organic and affiliate-driven discovery, and paid ads as something you turn on when you're ready. For this campaign, that line disappears. Platform placement now comes bundled with paid spend by default.

There's also a performance bar just to qualify for the campaign in the first place — several independent seller resources point to a Shop Performance Score around 3.5, though the exact metric and number have shifted more than once this year. Don't assume where you stand; pull your current number in Seller Center.

Three things worth 15 minutes this week, before the 11th:

→ Does each approved product already have an active GMV Max campaign — or is one about to get auto-created?
→ If one gets auto-created, what budget and bid does it run on by default, and would you actually choose those numbers?
→ Is your Shop Performance Score comfortably clear of the cutoff, not just technically above it?

Sellers running TikTok Shop behind Amazon or Walmart as a secondary channel are the most exposed here. A campaign you never turned on is easy to miss until the spend shows up.

Comment or DM if you want a second set of eyes on your GMV Max setup before the 11th.
