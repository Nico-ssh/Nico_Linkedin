## LINKEDIN POST

Three deadlines. Ten days. One of them was today.

Amazon's Black Friday / Cyber Monday inbound window isn't one date — it splits by how you ship:

→ AWD (Amazon Warehousing & Distribution) inventory: arrival deadline was October 14. Today.
→ FBA minimal-split shipments: October 21.
→ FBA Amazon-optimized-split shipments: October 28.

Different shipping method, different cutoff. If you've been working off "ship sometime in October," that's the wrong calendar for at least one of your SKUs.

Here's the part that catches even experienced sellers: arriving isn't the same as sellable. AWD stock still has to replenish into Amazon's fulfillment centers before it counts as available inventory — so the real safety margin is shorter than the arrival date makes it look.

There's a fourth date hiding in the same window, and it isn't an inventory deadline at all. If you want a product IN a Black Friday Week / Cyber Monday deal — not just in stock — that submission window closes October 20. Sellers who nailed their shipping timeline still miss this one, because it's never on the same checklist as inventory dates.

Three inbound paths. One deal deadline. All inside the next two weeks. None of them move, and Amazon doesn't send a second reminder.

Quick gut-check before you close the laptop:

→ Which method is this shipment actually using?
→ Is that method's deadline still ahead of you, or behind you?
→ If you're running a BF/CM deal, is it already submitted — or still sitting in draft?

Confirm your exact cutoff in Seller Central — account and region can shift it by a day or two.

The full breakdown — all three paths plus the deal deadline — is one image below. Save it before you need it.

Already past one of these dates for part of your catalog? That's worth a conversation before the gap turns into a stockout on Black Friday, not after.
