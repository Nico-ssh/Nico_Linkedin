## LINKEDIN POST

Tomorrow is the last day to submit a Black Friday Week deal on Amazon.

Most sellers running Sponsored Products into BFCM without one are about to pay full price for a worse number.

Amazon opened Black Friday Week and Cyber Monday deal submissions back on July 8. The window closes October 20 — tomorrow, as I'm writing this.

Here's why that date matters more than it looks.

A deal badge isn't just a discount sitting on the page. It's a conversion signal next to your ad. Front Row Group's analysis of its own BFCM 2024 client accounts (personal care and home brands) found deal-badged products gained roughly 7 points of conversion rate, versus about 1 point for items marked down the same amount but without a badge, and almost nothing for items with no discount at all.

Translate that into ad math: if your Sponsored Products traffic lands on a page without a badge, you're paying the same CPC as the badged listing next to you — during the highest-CPC week of the year — and converting at a lower rate. That's not a missed-promotion problem. That's a cost-per-sale problem.

Three things worth checking before the window closes:

1. Pull your top ad-spend ASINs and confirm a Black Friday Week / Cyber Monday deal is actually submitted for them — not a Prime Big Deal Days deal from October, which was a separate submission with its own window.
2. Confirm your account's exact cutoff in Seller Central rather than assuming "tomorrow" means the same hour for everyone. Deal windows have shifted by category before.
3. For any ASIN that won't make the deadline, build that into your target ACOS now. You'll be bidding against badged competitors with a conversion advantage you don't have.

The badge doesn't replace the bid. But bidding without one is a different, more expensive game.

Already submitted yours? Drop your category in the comments — curious how much the badge-vs-no-badge gap moves by vertical this year.
